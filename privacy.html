GymPit – Support & Privacy Support: pithealth@pm.me GymPit stores workout data locally on the user’s device. The developer does not receive this data, and the app uses no advertising or tracking. Apple Health access, file exports, and connections to a user-configured Healthpit server are optional and only activated by the user. In-app purchases are processed by Apple. Payment details are not shared with GymPit. Local data can be removed by deleting the app. Apple Health and Healthpit data must be deleted separately.
