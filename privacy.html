GymPit – Support & Privacy Support: pithealth@pm.me GymPit stores workout data locally on the user’s device. The developer does not receive this data, and the app uses no advertising or tracking. Apple Health access, file exports, and the connection to the user’s own Home Assistant instance are optional and only activated by the user. When enabled, selected workouts are sent to that instance and stored there by the HealthPit integration. The developer operates no server and receives no workout data. In-app purchases are processed by Apple. Payment details are not shared with GymPit. Local data can be removed by deleting the app. Data in Apple Health or in the user’s own Home Assistant instance must be deleted separately.
