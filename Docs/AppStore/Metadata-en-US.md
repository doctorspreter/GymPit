# App Store Metadata (en-US)

## Name

GymPit

## Subtitle

Workouts, sets, and progress

## Promotional text

Plan routines, log sets and rests, and follow your progress on iPhone and Apple
Watch.

## Description

GymPit supports your strength training without requiring an account or showing
advertising.

Create routines, customize exercises and equipment settings, and record weight,
repetitions, set types, RPE, rest periods, and notes. Completed workouts appear
in your history with volume, records, and progress.

During a workout, Live Activities keep the current set and rest timer visible.
The Apple Watch app can run a complete strength workout without an active iPhone
connection, including set controls, rest timers, live heart rate, active
calories, and duration. Finished sessions sync back to iPhone automatically.
GymPit can optionally save completed workouts to Apple Health.

Workout data stays on your device by default. CSV export, Apple Health, Huawei
Watch files, and the connection to your own Home Assistant instance are used
only when you choose to enable them.

GymPit is free. You can support continued development with an optional one-time
contribution processed by Apple.

## Review notes

No account is required for core features. Apple Health and the connection to a
self-hosted Home Assistant instance are optional. The four products under
**Settings > Support** are voluntary consumables that unlock no features. The
app remains fully functional without a purchase.
