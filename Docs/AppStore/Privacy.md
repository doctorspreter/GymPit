## Local workout data

GymPit stores routines, exercises, equipment settings, workout history, and app
settings locally on the device. A private App Group shares only the data needed
by the related Apple Watch app and Live Activity. GymPit contains no advertising
or tracking.

## Apple Health

After explicit authorization, GymPit can save completed workouts to Apple Health
and read existing workout data for reconciliation. The Apple Watch app can also
record duration, heart rate, and active calories during an active workout.
Health data is processed through Apple's HealthKit framework. Access can be
revoked at any time in system settings.

## Optional exports and connections

CSV and Huawei Watch files are created or imported only after an explicit user
action. GymPit connects to Home Assistant only after the user provides an
address and a long-lived access token. Selected workout data is then sent to the
user-operated Home Assistant instance and stored by the HealthPit integration.
The developer operates no server and receives none of this data. The token is
stored in the iOS keychain.

## Voluntary support

Apple processes one-time contributions as in-app purchases. GymPit does not
receive complete payment details. Apple's privacy policy also applies to payment
processing.

## Sharing and deletion

GymPit does not send workout data to the developer. Local data can be removed by
deleting the app. Data stored in Apple Health or the user's Home Assistant
instance must be deleted separately in those systems.

## Changes

This policy may be updated when features or legal requirements change.
